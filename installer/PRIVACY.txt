Mazraa - Privacy Policy
Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mazraa is a single-player farming game by Almadarek Games (publisher account: Sladewilsontv).

What we collect: nothing. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, location, contacts or any other personal
information, does not show third-party ads and does not sell or share data.

What stays on your PC: your save games, settings and a small local gameplay statistics file are stored only in your
Windows user folder (AppData). They never leave your computer. Uninstalling the game removes them (Microsoft Store
version) or you can delete the "Almadarek Games\Mazraa" folder under AppData\LocalLow.

Crash reports: the Unity engine may write a local crash log; it is not sent anywhere by the game.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.
