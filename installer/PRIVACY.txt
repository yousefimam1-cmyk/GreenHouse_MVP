Mazraa - Privacy Policy
Last updated: 17 October 2026

Mazraa is a farming game by Almadarek Games (publisher account: Sladewilsontv). You can play alone, or farm together
with up to 3 other players over the internet or on your local network (co-op).

What we collect: no personal information. The game does not ask for your real name, e-mail, phone number, location,
contacts or photos, has no accounts or passwords, does not show third-party ads and does not sell or share data.

What stays on your PC: your save games, settings, friends / blocked lists and a small local gameplay statistics file
are stored only in your Windows user folder (AppData). They never leave your computer. Uninstalling the game removes
them (Microsoft Store version) or you can delete the "Almadarek Games\Mazraa" folder under AppData\LocalLow.

Online co-op (only when you choose Multiplayer > Host an online game, Join with a code or Browse public games):
the game uses Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies (Authentication, Relay and Lobby) to connect players.
- Anonymous player ID: Unity creates a random anonymous player ID for your device. It is not linked to your name,
  e-mail or any account, and it is used only to connect you and to let a host keep a removed player out of the session.
- IP address: Unity's Relay and Lobby servers process your IP address to connect you to the other players, as any
  internet connection does. Other players never see your IP address in online games.
- Your player name is picked from a list of farm names (for example "Golden Palm 42"); you never type your real name.
- Public farms: if a host makes a farm public, its farm name, map, game version, number of players and join code are
  listed so other players can find it. Code-only and friends-only farms are not listed.
- Chat: messages you type in co-op are sent to the other players in that session through the host. A bad-word filter
  removes rude words, and you can turn chat off, allow friends only, block or report a player. The game does not keep
  chat on any server.
- Reports: when you report a player, the report (their player name and last few messages) is saved on your PC and an
  e-mail to us is prepared in your mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send yourself.
Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Local network (LAN) co-op connects directly to a friend's PC by its IP address on your own network; nothing goes
through our servers or Unity's.

Crash reports: the Unity engine may write a local crash log; it is not sent anywhere by the game.

Children: the game asks no one, including children, for personal information. Online chat can be turned off in
Multiplayer settings, and "Friends only" keeps strangers out of your farm and your chat.
